Figures are reproduced from the cited papers and project pages for commentary and remain the property of their authors. Numbers are as reported by each paper; several are self-evaluated or measured on different protocols, as noted on each page.

## Rules

- Number entries continuously across categories, and sort each category by release date.
- The hub figure is usually the paper's overview figure. Crop away white margins.
- Put the "Read more" link right after the paragraphs, above the image.
- Before publishing, re-check that each category still holds together. Merge or split categories that have one member or that mix designs.
